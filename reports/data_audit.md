## 2. Headline findings

1. **The two fuel-type files are byte-identical** (same SHA-256). `vehicle-registrations-by-fuel-type.csv` is a duplicate of `registrations-by-fuel-type.csv`; only the latter is used. Adding them together would double every figure.
2. **Aggregate leak in office `HR207`** (M/S Pasco Motors LLP, 40 Mile Stone(F.C), Haryana). In 29 months (Jan-2022 → May-2024) this single office reports 5,85,68,794 registrations — 31.45% of the whole fuel file. Its monthly value divided by the sum of every other office in India ranges 0.972–1.002 (median 1.001). It is a national total mis-recorded as an office row. Left in, it doubles national totals until May-2024 and creates a false ~50% "collapse" in June-2024. The next-largest office-month in India is 45,159 (Pune). **HR207 is excluded from the fuel and maker data.**
3. **BEV labels changed mid-series.** `Electric(BOV)` carries battery-electric volumes until early 2024; `Pure EV` appears in material volumes from March 2024 while `Electric(BOV)` falls. The combined series is continuous, so both labels are classified as battery-electric (BEV). Hybrid and fuel-cell labels are kept separate.
4. **The maker and vehicle-category files have no fuel field.** They cannot identify EVs. Their totals are all-fuel and are never presented as EV statistics.
5. **The vehicle-category file contains corrupted office rows** (aggregate values repeated across unrelated offices, e.g. identical six/seven-digit values in many offices in the same month). After the ETL outlier rule it is used only as all-fuel category context; see §6.
6. **The state charging snapshot sums to 29,277**, matching the published reference total of 29,277 (PIB annexure 'State-wise public EV charging stations' (Aug-2025), inputs from Ministry of Power; also reported from a Lok Sabha written reply by the MoS Heavy Industries.).
7. **The OMC year-wise file is a different, narrower measure** (stations installed by oil marketing companies). It is never added to the state snapshot.

- `total_installed` follows different rules in different rows: **installed = sum(FY)** for 34 rows; **installed = sum(FY) - decommissioned** for 3 rows (Arunachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Leh).
- `total_operational` is lower than `total_installed − decommissioned` in 36 rows, so some installed stations are neither operational nor decommissioned. The platform shows installations by FY with the source's labels and reports `total_operational` as given; it does not derive a utilisation figure.
- OMC stations are a subset of all public stations; the two files are never added together.

## 9. EV classification policy

- **EV (headline metric) = battery-electric vehicles (BEV)**: fuel labels `Electric(BOV)` and `Pure EV`.
- Reported separately, never added to BEV: `Strong Hybrid EV`, `Plug-in Hybrid EV`, `Fuel Cell Hydrogen`, and other hybrid labels (`Petrol/Hybrid` etc.).
- **All-vehicle denominator** for EV share = all fuel labels in the fuel file (including `Not Applicable`), excluding HR207.
- Full mapping: `data/reference/fuel_classification.csv`.

## 10. Data-quality rules applied by the ETL

1. Use only `registrations-by-fuel-type.csv` as the fuel source; the byte-identical copy is ignored (checked by hash on every run).
2. Exclude office HR207 from fuel and maker data (aggregate leak).
3. Standardise state names through `data/reference/state_mapping.csv`; unknown names fail the pipeline.
4. Key offices by (state, office code, office name) so West Bengal offices with null codes are kept and not merged.
5. Sum exact natural-key duplicates rather than dropping them (they are separate source rows with the same key); counts are reported.
6. Category file: null a value when it is ≥1,000 and either repeated identically in ≥3 offices in the same month and vehicle type, or >10× that office's median for the vehicle type. Result validated against the fuel file.
7. Never interpolate or impute missing months.
